About BRUNC

We build the conversations your business doesn't have time to have.

BRUNC AI Solutions Ltd builds AI voice agents, chatbots, and automations for businesses, hospitals, SACCOs, and institutions — anywhere calls and queries outpace the people answering them.

Why we exist

Every business loses something to the gap between a question and an answer — a missed call, a slow reply, a customer who moves on before anyone gets back to them. That gap is rarely about effort. It's about hours in the day.

BRUNC exists to close that gap. We build agents that know your business — your policies, your systems, your customers' actual questions — so the answer arrives the moment it's asked, in the language they're comfortable speaking, at any hour.

We started by proving this could work in a real, high-volume environment, and built it to scale across any organization facing the same pressure — from clinics to SACCOs to enterprise support desks.
